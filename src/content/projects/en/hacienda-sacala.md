## The place

Hacienda Sacalá is in Izamal, Yucatán, a pueblo mágico of yellow streets and long history.

It brings together a golf course, a boutique hotel and a neighbourhood with the cooking, culture and art of the region. Muñoz Arquitectos and AS Arquitectura designed it, with the stone and the light of the place as materials.

It is a way of living and resting in a place with memory.

## Where it is

Izamal lies in the centre of Yucatán, between Mérida and Valladolid, inland. It is one of the oldest towns on the peninsula: a Franciscan convent built on a Mayan pyramid, streets painted the same yellow, and a life around the plaza that has not gone away.

Hacienda Sacalá is on the edge of town, on the land of a former henequen hacienda, with the Yucatán plain around it and the big sky only the interior has.

## How it is laid out

Three things live together at Sacalá and each has its place. The golf course takes the open part of the land, between lagoons and the low vegetation of the plain. The boutique hotel stands where the hacienda house stood, within its stone walls. And the neighbourhood is laid out in short streets around both.

The neighbourhood is not a subdivision: it is an extension of Izamal, with the cooking, culture and art of the region on its main street, so that whoever lives there has a town's life and whoever stays at the hotel has a town to step out into.

The architects worked with what was there: the local limestone, the existing walls, the raking light of the Yucatán afternoon.

## How you live here

Izamal has a rhythm you notice on arrival: nobody hurries. Mornings are cool and golf is played early; midday is for shade and a long lunch; the afternoon is for walking the town and sitting in the plaza.

At Sacalá you live at that cadence and with what a pueblo mágico offers: festivals, a market, music, and a Yucatecan cooking that here is still home cooking.

Timeless beauty is the sentence this page closes on. In Izamal, timeless is not a figure of speech.

## How you buy here

Izamal is in the interior of Yucatán, outside the coastal strip Mexican law reserves. Here foreign buyers purchase directly, with the deed in their own name, and no trust is needed. It is the same ease that makes Mérida a destination for so many foreigners, in a smaller and older town.

The notary gives the sale its legal form and registers the deed in the Public Registry. Before you sign, we give you a written estimate of closing costs, and we tell you what is available in the neighbourhood with the plan in hand.

## Questions

### Where is Izamal and how do I get there?
In the centre of Yucatán, inland, between Mérida and Valladolid. You drive there on the highway from Mérida, whose airport is international. It is one of the oldest towns on the peninsula and is designated a pueblo mágico.

### What is at Hacienda Sacalá?
A golf course, a boutique hotel within the walls of the old hacienda, and a neighbourhood of short streets with the cooking, culture and art of the region. Each has its place and the three support each other.

### Can I buy a house or a lot?
The Sacalá neighbourhood is laid out in lots for houses. What is available changes over time; when we speak we tell you what there is, with the plan in hand, and how the project's architects work.

### Can I buy as a foreigner, without a trust?
Yes. Izamal is in the interior of Yucatán, outside the reserved coastal strip, so foreign buyers purchase directly, with the deed in their own name.

### Who designed Hacienda Sacalá?
Muñoz Arquitectos and AS Arquitectura, working with the local limestone, the hacienda's existing walls and the light of the Yucatán afternoon as their materials.

### Is it a place to live or to rest?
Both. The neighbourhood is for living at a town's pace; the hotel is for those who come to rest. The main street, with its cooking and its culture, is where the two lives meet.
