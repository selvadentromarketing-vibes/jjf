## The place

Selvadentro sits in the heart of the Tulum jungle. Here the architecture does not impose itself: it shows through the native vegetation, the cenotes and the open canopy.

We conceived it around three things: privacy, wellbeing and a deep respect for the land. The residences are woven into the jungle rather than cut out of it.

It is an invitation to live in harmony with nature without giving up comfort or the well-made detail.

## Where it is

Selvadentro is inland, in the jungle of Tulum, in the state of Quintana Roo. It is not on the beach and not inside the hotel zone: it is in the bush, where the jungle is still jungle and the water runs under the limestone. You arrive by road from town, and the last stretch of the drive is already part of the place.

That distance is deliberate. The Tulum coast has been built fast; the interior keeps the silence, the shade and the fresh water that made this region what it is. Selvadentro is there for the person looking for exactly that.

## How it is laid out

The masterplan divides the land into sectors named for what lives in it: Cenote, Mono, Toh and Jaguar. Within each sector the lots sit along looping lanes, so that no house faces another head-on and the jungle stays between all of them.

A trail crosses the whole development from one end to the other. It links the shared spaces, passes beside the water and is walked under the canopy. It is the most-used part of the place and the least visible from the air.

The cenotes are inside the community. They are not an annex or a viewpoint: they are the center everything else was planned around. The density is low for that reason, not as a matter of taste.

## How you live here

A house in Selvadentro is designed to disappear a little. The volumes rest in the clearing that already existed, the large trees are kept, and shade does half the climate's work. The light that arrives is filtered light.

Days start early, with the birds, and end with the jungle sounding. There is cold water a few steps away. The noise of town does not reach you. What reaches you is what was already there.

We put it this way: we do not sell properties, we design refuges. Selvadentro is where that sentence becomes literal.

## How you buy here

Tulum lies within the coastal strip Mexican law reserves, and there foreign buyers purchase through a bank trust, the fideicomiso: a Mexican bank is the formal titleholder and you are the beneficiary, with every right to use, rent, sell and bequeath. It is the same instrument used along the whole Riviera Maya, and we explain it calmly on the how-it-works page.

If you are Mexican, you buy directly before a notary. Either way, before you sign anything, we give you a written estimate of closing costs and a payment schedule.

What is available changes over time. When we speak we tell you exactly what there is, with its plan and its price, in your language.

## Questions

### Can I buy in Selvadentro if I am not Mexican?
Yes. Tulum is in the coastal strip reserved by Mexican law, so foreign buyers acquire through a trust with a Mexican bank. You are the beneficiary, with the right to use, rent, sell and inherit. The notary and the trustee bank handle the paperwork; we walk with you through every step.

### What do you buy in Selvadentro, a lot or a finished house?
The masterplan orders Selvadentro into residential lots within the jungle, each meant for a house of your own. What is available in each sector changes over time, so we tell you precisely when we speak, with the plan in hand.

### Are there cenotes inside the community?
Yes. The cenotes are inside the development and are the center everything else was planned around. One of the plan's sectors carries their name. The water is fresh, cold and a few steps from the houses.

### How dense is Selvadentro?
It is a low-density community by design. The lots sit along looping lanes so the jungle stays between all the houses, the large trees are kept, and the buildings rest in the clearings that already existed.

### Where exactly is it, and how do I get there?
Inland, in the Tulum jungle, outside the hotel zone. You arrive by road from town. We send you the exact location and arrange the visit with you; almost all our buyers come once or twice before deciding.

### What happens to the jungle when you build?
Each house is designed for its clearing. The volumes rest where there was already room, the native vegetation is kept, and the canopy's shade is part of the project. We say it in our vision and we keep to it on site: the residences are woven into the jungle rather than cut out of it.
