## The place

Aldea Zama is a master-planned community in Tulum that now has a life of its own. It joins the Mayan heritage of the place with an international, contemporary way of living.

Private residential quarters, commercial streets with movement, and an infrastructure considered from the first line of the plan.

It is proof that a master plan can become a town with soul.

## Where it is

Aldea Zama lies between Tulum town and the hotel zone, on the stretch that joins them. From here you walk or cycle to both: to the center one way, to the beach the other. That position explains almost everything else about it — it is the point where Tulum's daily life and its coast touch.

Which is why the masterplan called it, from the start, the heart of Tulum. Not as a promise, but as a description of where it stands.

## How it is laid out

The masterplan is clear about what belongs to whom. There are lots for houses, lots for apartment buildings, commercial frontages on the main streets, mixed-use stretches with shops below and homes above, and a strip set aside for hotels. Between them, green areas that are not for sale.

The streets were drawn before the buildings. That is why Aldea Zama has what almost no development in Tulum has: continuous pavements, roundabouts, street trees and services underground.

It was built in phases. Some are finished neighborhoods now, with neighbours and shops open; others are under way and follow the same layout. The availability plan shows which lots remain in each.

## How you live here

Aldea Zama lives like a small town. You go down to the street, buy bread, say hello. Restaurants, cafés and shops are a stroll away, and the beach is a bicycle ride away.

The residential quarters are private and quiet; the commercial streets have just enough movement. That mix is what makes the place work all year, not only in season.

Here you live with purpose: it is a finished community you can live in today, not a drawing of what will be.

## How you buy here

Tulum lies within the coastal strip Mexican law reserves, and there foreign buyers purchase through a bank trust, the fideicomiso. A Mexican bank is the formal titleholder and you are the beneficiary, with every right to use, rent, sell and bequeath. We explain it step by step on the how-it-works page.

If you are Mexican, you buy directly before a notary. In Aldea Zama there are both lots to build on and properties already built, so it is worth talking first about what you are looking for.

## Questions

### Where is Aldea Zama relative to the beach and the town?
Between the two. Aldea Zama occupies the stretch between Tulum town and the hotel zone, and you reach both on foot or by bicycle. That position is what gives it its character as a town: close to everything, without being on the highway.

### What can you buy in Aldea Zama?
The masterplan includes residential lots, lots for apartment buildings, commercial frontages, mixed-use stretches and a hotel strip. What remains available changes over time; when we speak we tell you exactly what there is, with the plan in hand.

### Is Aldea Zama finished or still under construction?
Both, in phases. There are finished neighborhoods, with neighbours and shops open, and there are phases under way that follow the same layout. You can live in Aldea Zama today.

### Can I buy if I am a foreigner?
Yes. Tulum is in the reserved coastal strip, so foreign buyers purchase through a trust with a Mexican bank, with the right to use, rent, sell and inherit. It is the same instrument used along the whole Riviera Maya.

### Can a property in Aldea Zama be rented out?
Yes. The mix of shops, hotels and homes gives Aldea Zama life all year, and many properties are rented seasonally or long-term. We guide you on what applies to each zone.

### What services does it have?
The streets were drawn before the buildings: continuous pavements, street trees, roundabouts and underground services. On the commercial streets there are restaurants, cafés and shops already open.
