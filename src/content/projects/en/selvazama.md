## The place

Selvazama lies in the hotel zone of Tulum, between the jungle and the sea. It brings residential, hotel, commercial and cultural life onto one territory, with the jungle as the thread.

Homes, hotels, a shopping street and a school share the ground with the trees that were there first.

It keeps growing in phases, with the idea it began with: build with the jungle, not over it.

## Where it is

Selvazama is in Tulum's hotel zone, on the jungle side. The sea is a step away, across the coastal road; behind it, the jungle runs as far as you can see. It is the strip that made Tulum known, and also the one that asks for the most care.

So the land was treated as what it is: a piece of jungle with access to the sea, not a beachfront lot. The difference lies in how much was kept.

## How it is laid out

The masterplan orders the territory by uses that support each other. Homes in the quietest part; hotels toward the front; a shopping street where everyone meets; and a school, because a place with children in it is a place where people actually live.

The blocks follow the shape of the jungle, not a grid. The lanes go around the large trees rather than through them, and the shared spaces sit where there were already clearings.

Each phase opens when the previous one is finished. So the place grows the way a town grows, not the way a building site does.

## How you live here

In Selvazama you live with the sea close and the jungle overhead. In the morning you can walk to the beach; in the afternoon the shade of the trees drops the temperature several degrees without anyone switching anything on.

There are neighbours who live here all year and visitors who stay for a season. The school and the shopping street make the two lives cross.

Living nature, and a deep respect for the surroundings: it is the sentence this page closes on, and it is what you see when you arrive.

## How you buy here

Tulum lies within the coastal strip Mexican law reserves, and there foreign buyers purchase through a trust with a Mexican bank, the fideicomiso. You are the beneficiary, with the right to use, rent, sell and bequeath. We explain it calmly on the how-it-works page.

If you are Mexican, you buy directly before a notary. Selvazama has different uses and different phases, so the first thing is to talk about what you are looking for: a house, a lot, a space for a business.

## Questions

### Is Selvazama on the beach?
It is in Tulum's hotel zone, on the jungle side, with the sea a step away across the coastal road. It was treated as a piece of jungle with access to the sea, and that decision is what sets it apart.

### What is in Selvazama besides houses?
Hotels, a shopping street and a school, alongside the residential zones. The masterplan orders them so they support each other and so the place has life all year round.

### Can I buy a lot, or only a finished house?
It depends on the phase. There are zones where you buy land to build on and zones that are already built. When we speak we tell you what is available, with the plan in hand.

### Can I buy if I am a foreigner?
Yes. Tulum is in the coastal strip reserved by Mexican law, so foreign buyers purchase through a trust with a Mexican bank, with every right to use, rent, sell and inherit.

### How is the jungle protected during construction?
The blocks follow the shape of the land, the lanes go around the large trees, and the shared spaces occupy the clearings that already existed. Each phase opens when the previous one is finished, so the place grows like a town and not like a building site.

### Is there a school?
Yes. The school has been part of the plan from the beginning, because a place with children in it is a place where people live, not only where they spend a season.
