JJF Creando is responsible for the personal data you provide to us, under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties and its Regulations.

## What we collect
Name, phone, email, preferred language and the development you are interested in, when you write to us through this site, on WhatsApp or when scheduling a call. Also technical browsing data, such as your IP address and browser, through measurement tools.

## What we use it for
To answer you, share information about our developments, schedule visits and calls, and follow up on your interest. Secondarily, to measure how this site and our ads perform, and to improve how we communicate. You may object to the secondary purposes by writing to us.

## Who we share it with
With the providers that help us operate — contact management, scheduling, measurement and hosting — only for the purposes described. We do not sell your data.

To measure our ads on ChatGPT, when you write to us through the form we share with OpenAI your email turned into an irreversible code (SHA-256), your IP address and your browser details. We never share your name or phone number. You may object to this use by writing to us.

## Your rights
You may access, rectify, cancel or object to the processing of your data, and withdraw your consent. We answer within the periods set by law.

## Changes to this notice
Any change will be published on this page with its update date.
